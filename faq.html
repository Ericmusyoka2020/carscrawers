Frequently Asked Questions

How many free posts can I create?

Each seller starts with 6 free posts. Paid and gifted posts appear separately in your account.

How do I upload photos?

Upload at least 1 photo and no more than 5 photos for each post.

How are reports reviewed?

Every report goes directly to Wariara Cars Central Rift for post review. A report requires a written reason.

How do sellers get paid or advertise?

Create a seller account, then choose an available post plan or account payment option. Post payments use M-Pesa.
